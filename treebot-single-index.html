On Android Chrome, open the hosted HTTPS site, then use browser menu → Add to Home screen. Camera, GPS, and AI require permissions and network access as applicable. Saved tree metadata and compressed thumbnails stay in your browser's IndexedDB. This single-file test is not a full offline-installable PWA.
